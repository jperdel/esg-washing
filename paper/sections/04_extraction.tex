\section{Document extraction: the lexical ESG-zone filter}
\label{sec:extraction}

The original study vectorises each report in its entirety: a standalone
sustainability report is, by construction, already ESG text, so
\citet{lagasio2024} needs no step to separate ESG content from anything else.
Our corpus is different by design (\S\ref{sec:scope}, \S\ref{sec:data}): annual
reports and URDs in which ESG disclosure is embedded inside a much larger
financial and legal document. Feeding the whole report to the downstream
measures would let financial-statement bulk and document length dominate
every score. Extraction is therefore a required stage with no counterpart in
the original, and it fixes the denominator against which the length effects
of \S\ref{sec:measurement} and \S\ref{sec:artefact} operate.

\subsection{Paragraph segmentation}

Each PDF is read with PyMuPDF's block-level extractor
(\texttt{page.get\_text("blocks")}), text blocks only, sorted top-to-bottom,
left-to-right, by \texttt{src/lexical\_document\_filter.py}
(\texttt{LexicalDocumentFilter}), whose full parameter and dependency
listing is in \S\ref{sec:repro}. A block is accepted as a paragraph only
after passing four filters, in order: (i) \emph{margin strip} — dropped if entirely in the top
or bottom margin band ($y_1 < 0.08h$ or $y_0 > 0.92h$, $h$ = page height);
(ii) \emph{running header/footer suppression} — every margin-band block of
at most 120 characters is normalised (digits $\to$ \texttt{\#}) and counted
across the document's pages, and any normalised string recurring on more
than 30\% of pages is blacklisted and dropped wherever it occurs; (iii)
\emph{financial-table detection} — a block is dropped as tabular only if it
contains \emph{no} ESG keyword (the regex defined below) \emph{and} either
its digit-to-character ratio exceeds 0.35 with at most 6 lines, or every
line is under 60 characters, there are at least two lines, and the first has
no terminal punctuation (period, exclamation mark, question mark, or colon);
the keyword veto keeps a table row that happens to carry ESG content rather
than discarding it as noise; (iv) \emph{section headers} — an all-caps block
(ignoring non-letters), at least two letters, at most 10 tokens, under 60\%
single-character tokens, with no token matching a bare-filename pattern (a
run of word characters, a period, then a 2--6 character suffix, e.g.\
\texttt{report.pdf}), is emitted as a \texttt{\S~TITLE~\S} marker that tags
subsequent paragraphs with their section, rather than entering the
paragraph stream.
Surviving text has URLs stripped, hyphenated line breaks rejoined, non-ASCII
characters dropped, and whitespace collapsed.

\subsection{Density scoring and zone construction}

Each paragraph $p$ is scored by keyword density,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{density}(p) = 100 \times \frac{\mathrm{kw\_count}(p)}{\mathrm{words}(p)},
\label{eq:kw-density}
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{kw\_count}(p)$ counts matches, on the raw paragraph text, of a
single compiled regex combining the 154-term vocabulary of \S\ref{sec:lexicon}
— hyphen/space-interchangeable, pluralisable, sorted longest-first so that
\emph{carbon neutrality} is not shadowed by \emph{carbon} — with 24
additional patterns not expressible as flat terms: morphological families
(\texttt{sustainab\textbackslash w+}, \dots), digit-bearing terms
(\texttt{co2e?}, \texttt{scope\,[123]}, ISO codes), and collocations that
disambiguate polysemous heads (\emph{social}, \emph{water}, \emph{energy})
from their non-ESG senses.

A paragraph is \emph{hot} if $\mathrm{density}(p) \geq 1.0$. Every hot
paragraph is expanded to a window of one paragraph either side; overlapping
or adjacent windows ($\mathrm{lo} \leq \mathrm{prev\_hi}+1$) are merged into
one contiguous zone; merged zones under 150 characters are dropped. Table
statistics (keyword count, word count, density) are recomputed on each
surviving zone's concatenated text, not summed from its paragraphs.
Table~\ref{tab:extraction-params} lists the free parameters governing hot-paragraph
detection and zone construction, none tuned by grid search; the thresholds internal to
financial-table and section-header detection above are additional constants, given in
prose rather than tabulated. Per PDF, zones are sorted by start offset and joined
into one \texttt{relevant\_text} field, which is all that every later stage
(\S\ref{sec:lexicon} onward) reads.

\subsection{Design rationale}

The filter is a pure function of the PDF and the lexicon files: no network
call, no external API, no random seed on this path. This matters directly
for the determinism claimed in \S\ref{sec:repro}. An earlier version of the
pipeline extracted fixed-size chunks (1,200 characters, 300-character
overlap) and selected among them with a semantic filter — cosine
similarity, in an embedding space from a hosted model (Vertex AI
\texttt{text-embedding-004}), against hand-written anchor queries. That
filter was removed and replaced by the deterministic lexical filter above,
together with every remaining dependency on the hosted embedding service;
the repository records what changed but not why, so no claim is made here
about the original motivation. The methodological case for the replacement
stands independently of that history: a filter behind a paid, versioned,
externally hosted model cannot satisfy a full-determinism claim, because the
model can change or be deprecated independently of this codebase and the
same PDF is not guaranteed to yield the same selection on a later run.

\subsection{Recall is bounded by the lexicon}

Extraction's matching set is a strict superset of the measurement
vocabulary: the 24 extra patterns above — morphological families,
digit-bearing terms, four precision collocations for polysemous heads, and
\emph{paris agreement} and \emph{just transition}, the two phrases
\S\ref{sec:lexicon} records as removed by hand from the 154-term list because
they collapse under lemmatisation — can make a paragraph hot and pull a zone
in, but are not part of the fixed 154-term count that \SUS\ itself tallies.
Two bounds follow, not one: a term outside extraction's combined set triggers
no hot paragraph and enters no zone, so it is invisible everywhere; a term
inside that set but outside the 154 — \emph{paris agreement} is the documented
case — can still reach a zone without ever being counted by \SUS, so its
own recall is bounded by the narrower list even for text the filter has
already admitted. \S\ref{sec:limitations} develops the consequences; it is
noted here as a direct property of this step.
